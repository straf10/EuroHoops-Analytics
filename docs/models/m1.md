## What it does
M1 rates every team's offence, defence and pace from box-score possessions and turns the ratings
into a full predictive distribution for each game: expected points of each side, a margin
distribution (so P(home win) = P(margin > 0)) and a totals distribution. It is compared with a
MOV-Elo re-tuned on exactly the same tuning seasons, on validation seasons neither model was
tuned on, and it runs live next to (never instead of) the pre-registered Elo log.

## Data and the excluded share
- **Rows:** `team_games` in the marts, one row per team per rated game (played, not a forfeit),
  from the raw cache only: EuroLeague box JSON totals (incl. team rebounds and turnovers), ESAKE
  totals rows, and the BasketHotel play-by-play for the 2018-20 GBL games whose ESAKE box does
  not reproduce the result (`docs/data/possessions.md`).
- **Seasons (E-a):** EuroLeague warm-up 2007-08 to 2014-15, tuning 2015-16 to 2022-23,
  validation 2023-24, test 2024-25 and 2025-26. GBL warm-up 2018-19 to 2020-21, tuning 2021-22
  and 2022-23, validation 2023-24, test 2024-25 and 2025-26. Fits always use every earlier game.
- **Excluded:** games without box lines are still forecast and scored but never feed a fit. In
  the scored seasons that is 2 tuning games per competition and none in validation or test (see
  "Numbers"; the full list with reasons is the `team_games_missing` mart table: 9 games over
  both competitions, 5 of them live 2026-27 EuroLeague games not cached yet).

## Possessions and their validation
Per team `FGA - OREB + TOV + 0.42 FTA`; the game's possessions are the mean of both teams, and
pace is possessions per 40 minutes. The weight is the one that makes the formula match a
play-by-play possession counter on average: `w = Σ(PBP - (FGA - OREB + TOV)) / Σ FTA`. On the
seeded 50-game EuroLeague sample that is 0.421; on every cached EuroLeague game from 2011-12 it
is 0.4155 (0.4371 in 2011-14, 0.4093 from 2015-16); 0.42 is the rounded value, fixed for every
season (`docs/data/possessions.md`). At 0.42 the counter agrees within 2 for 0.940 of the
sample's team-games (target 0.9; below it at v1's 0.44) and 0.902 of all team-games. The GBL uses
the same weight. The stints mart (built and validated, not used by M1) passes the four lineup
checks in 0.9587 of 2011-14 games and 0.9894 from 2015-16 on.

## Equations
Ratings, a weighted ridge on team-game rows (points per 100 possessions):

    100 * points / poss_game = mu + h * home + off[team] - def[opponent]

home = +1 for the home side's row, -1 for the away side's, 0 at a neutral venue. Row weight =
`poss_game * 0.5^(age / half_life) * carry^(seasons back)`: exponential decay plus an extra pull
toward the league mean at each season start. mu and h are unpenalised; off/def carry a ridge
penalty in possessions of evidence. Pace: `poss_game * 40 / minutes = mu_p + pace[home] +
pace[away]`, same decay, penalty in games. For home H and away A with expected pace P:

    ORtg_H = mu + h + off[H] - def[A]      ORtg_A = mu - h + off[A] - def[H]
    margin = P (ORtg_H - ORtg_A) / 100     total = P (ORtg_H + ORtg_A) / 100

Every game is forecast from the fit on games that tipped off before its round's first tip-off
(walk-forward by round; `tests/test_leakage.py` proves forecasts before T are bit-identical under
any edit at or after T). The margin is `m + s * e`, e standard Normal or Student-t(df), with s
constant or `s * sqrt(P / mean P)`; the total is Normal with a tuning-RMS sigma.

## Chosen variant and why
Four margin variants were declared before any v1 validation run (`reports/week5-7_progress.md`):
Normal or Student-t, constant or pace-scaled scale. The grids (v2 rating: half-life x carry x
ridge, 168 points; pace: 144 points) and each variant's df and scale are fitted on tuning seasons
only. The gate uses the variant with the best **tuning** log loss: `student_t_const` for the
EuroLeague (df 7) and `student_t_pace` for the GBL (df 20). Every variant's validation and test
numbers are in "Numbers"; the four are within a few thousandths of each other in log loss. No
variant was added after the first validation run.

**Grid edges.** v1's EuroLeague rating half-life (480 d, the maximum) and ridge (250, the
minimum), EuroLeague pace half-life and ridge, and GBL rating ridge (250) sat on their grid
edges. The v2 grids reach 1920 d / 62.5 (rating) and 30 d / 0.5 (pace). The pace values are now
interior; the EuroLeague rating moved to half-life 1920 d (the new maximum) with carry 0.25 (the
minimum), i.e. almost no decay within a span of seasons but a strong pull to the mean at each
season start, and the GBL rating ridge to 62.5 (the new minimum). The tuning log loss is flat
along those edges (EuroLeague 0.6065 in v2), so the grids are not widened again.

## Results
**Gate (validation 2023-24, M1 minus the comparison Elo, paired bootstrap 95% CI):**
- EuroLeague: log loss 0.5877 vs 0.5885, difference -0.0008 [-0.0077, 0.0071]: **pass** on the
  rule, not significant. Margin CRPS -0.023 [-0.085, 0.043]; totals CRPS -1.011 [-1.393, -0.609]
  (Elo has no totals model; it uses the two-season mean).
- GBL: log loss 0.4298 vs 0.4362, difference -0.0065 [-0.0370, 0.0208]: **pass**, not
  significant. For the GBL the spread and total errors say more than win-probability log loss
  (many lopsided games): margin CRPS -0.218 [-0.437, 0.003] (not significant), totals CRPS
  -0.880 [-1.429, -0.335].

**Test (2024-25 and 2025-26, scored once after the v2 verdict was committed):**
- EuroLeague: log loss 0.6232 vs 0.6246, difference -0.0013 [-0.0072, 0.0052].
- GBL: log loss 0.4835 vs 0.4799, difference 0.0037 [-0.0262, 0.0330]: on test M1
  is still *worse* than Elo in log loss, within noise.

## Calibration
Validation ECE: EuroLeague 0.045 (Elo 0.057), GBL 0.077 (Elo 0.095). The ten reliability bins
of every split and model are in the reports (`metrics.<split>.<model>.reliability`).

## Heteroscedasticity (R4)
On the tuning seasons the squared margin residual does not grow with expected pace or with the
rating gap: EuroLeague pace slope p = 0.192, gap slope p = 0.209, likelihood-ratio test of a
pace-scaled sigma p = 0.233; GBL p = 0.570, 0.394 and 0.642. This matches R4's weak evidence: a
constant scale is enough, and the pace-scaled GBL variant won on tuning log loss by far less
than the noise. The heavier-tailed Student-t is what helps in the EuroLeague.

## Where M1 loses, limitations and what failed
- **Early rounds (EuroLeague):** validation rounds 1-5 log loss 0.5947 vs Elo 0.5784, and the
  postseason 0.6795 vs 0.6770: the season-start carry-over pulls M1 further toward the mean than
  Elo's reversion. M1 wins later in the regular season (0.5777 vs 0.5818).
- **Lopsided GBL games:** 0.2817 vs Elo 0.2733 on the games Elo gives 75% or more to the
  favourite; M1 wins the balanced ones (0.6907 vs 0.7235). M1's margins for the
  Olympiacos/Panathinaikos blowouts are more cautious than Elo's.
- **Not significant:** no log-loss gain is distinguishable from zero in either competition, on
  validation or on test, and the GBL test goes the other way. M1 is live because the
  pre-registered rule says so (E-g), not because it is proven better.
- **Post-hoc v2:** see "Versions"; v2 is slightly worse than v1 on the EuroLeague test and
  slightly better on the GBL test, all inside the noise. v2 went live as declared, not by
  picking the better version on test.
- **Possessions:** one free-throw weight for all seasons although the EuroLeague value drifts
  (0.4371 in 2011-14, 0.4093 from 2015-16), the GBL borrowing the EuroLeague weight, and ESAKE
  2018-19 to 2020-21 totals without team rebounds and turnovers (about +0.8 possessions per
  team); all are level shifts.
- **Live window:** the live M1 fits from 2023-24 (EuroLeague) and 2018-19 (GBL), the seasons
  the daily workflow holds, not from 2007. With the long v2 EuroLeague half-life the decay no
  longer discounts old games; the season carry (0.25 per season start) does.
- **One home effect** for all seasons (no special 2020-21 no-fans term); no roster information.
