# Model card: M1, the possession-based team model

*Weeks 5–7 (PLAN §5.2). Code: `src/eurohoops/models/team_eff.py`, `src/eurohoops/eval/m1_backtest.py`,
`src/eurohoops/live_m1.py`. Reports: `reports/backtest_m1.json` (EuroLeague),
`reports/backtest_m1_gbl.json` (GBL). Every number below is copied from those reports (or from
`reports/possessions.json` / `reports/stints_mart.json`): the tables in "Numbers" name the JSON
path of each value, and `tests/test_model_card.py` checks every table value, and every decimal
in the prose, against the reports.*

## What it does
M1 rates every team's offence, defence and pace from box-score possessions and turns the ratings
into a full predictive distribution for each game: expected points of each side, a margin
distribution (so P(home win) = P(margin > 0)) and a totals distribution. It is compared with a
MOV-Elo re-tuned on exactly the same tuning seasons, on validation seasons neither model was
tuned on, and it runs live next to (never instead of) the pre-registered Elo log.

## Data and the excluded share
- **Rows:** `team_games` in the marts, one row per team per rated game (played, not a forfeit),
  from the raw cache only: EuroLeague box JSON totals (incl. team rebounds and turnovers), ESAKE
  totals rows, and the BasketHotel play-by-play for the 2018-20 GBL games whose ESAKE box does
  not reproduce the result (`docs/data/possessions.md`).
- **Seasons (E-a):** EuroLeague warm-up 2007-08 to 2014-15, tuning 2015-16 to 2022-23,
  validation 2023-24, test 2024-25 and 2025-26. GBL warm-up 2018-19 to 2020-21, tuning 2021-22
  and 2022-23, validation 2023-24, test 2024-25 and 2025-26. Fits always use every earlier game.
- **Excluded:** games without box lines are still forecast and scored but never feed a fit. In
  the scored seasons that is 2 tuning games per competition and none in validation or test (see
  "Numbers"; the full list with reasons is the `team_games_missing` mart table: 9 games over
  both competitions, 5 of them live 2026-27 EuroLeague games not cached yet).

## Possessions and their validation
Per team `FGA - OREB + TOV + 0.44 FTA`; the game's possessions are the mean of both teams, and
pace is possessions per 40 minutes. On the seeded 50-game EuroLeague sample a play-by-play
possession counter agrees within 2 for 0.890 of team-games (target 0.9). The 0.44 free-throw
weight is an NBA estimate; the weight the EuroLeague play-by-play implies (0.421) gives 0.940.
The formula is kept as R1 specifies: the bias is a near-constant level shift that the league
mean absorbs (`docs/data/possessions.md`). The stints mart (built and validated, not used by
M1) passes the four lineup checks in 0.9587 of 2011-14 games and 0.9894 from 2015-16 on.

## Equations
Ratings, a weighted ridge on team-game rows (points per 100 possessions):

    100 * points / poss_game = mu + h * home + off[team] - def[opponent]

home = +1 for the home side's row, -1 for the away side's, 0 at a neutral venue. Row weight =
`poss_game * 0.5^(age / half_life) * carry^(seasons back)`: exponential decay plus an extra pull
toward the league mean at each season start. mu and h are unpenalised; off/def carry a ridge
penalty in possessions of evidence. Pace: `poss_game * 40 / minutes = mu_p + pace[home] +
pace[away]`, same decay, penalty in games. For home H and away A with expected pace P:

    ORtg_H = mu + h + off[H] - def[A]      ORtg_A = mu - h + off[A] - def[H]
    margin = P (ORtg_H - ORtg_A) / 100     total = P (ORtg_H + ORtg_A) / 100

Every game is forecast from the fit on games that tipped off before its round's first tip-off
(walk-forward by round; `tests/test_leakage.py` proves forecasts before T are bit-identical under
any edit at or after T). The margin is `m + s * e`, e standard Normal or Student-t(df), with s
constant or `s * sqrt(P / mean P)`; the total is Normal with a tuning-RMS sigma.

## Chosen variant and why
Four margin variants were declared before any validation run (`reports/week5-7_progress.md`):
Normal or Student-t, constant or pace-scaled scale. The grids (rating: half-life x carry x ridge,
80 points; pace: 64 points) and each variant's df and scale were fitted on tuning seasons only.
The gate uses the variant with the best **tuning** log loss: `student_t_const` for the
EuroLeague and `student_t_pace` for the GBL, both with df 7. Every variant's validation and test
numbers are in "Numbers"; the four are within a few ten-thousandths of each other in log loss.
No variant was added after the first validation run (none is post-hoc). Several tuned values sit
on the edge of their declared grid (EuroLeague rating half-life at the maximum and ridge at the
minimum; GBL rating ridge at the minimum); the grids were kept as declared.

## Results
**Gate (validation 2023-24, M1 minus the comparison Elo, paired bootstrap 95% CI):**
- EuroLeague: log loss 0.5872 vs 0.5885, difference -0.0013 [-0.0084, 0.0064]: **pass** on the
  pre-registered rule, not significant. Margin CRPS -0.026 [-0.092, 0.045]; totals CRPS
  -1.007 [-1.385, -0.606] (Elo has no totals model; it uses the two-season mean).
- GBL: log loss 0.4328 vs 0.4362, difference -0.0035 [-0.0356, 0.0242]: **pass**, not
  significant. For the GBL the spread and total errors say more than win-probability log loss
  (many lopsided games): margin CRPS -0.172 [-0.419, 0.072] (not significant), totals CRPS
  -0.835 [-1.295, -0.388].

**Test (2024-25 and 2025-26, scored once after the verdict was committed):**
- EuroLeague: log loss 0.6220 vs 0.6246, difference -0.0025 [-0.0077, 0.0027].
- GBL: log loss 0.4875 vs 0.4799, difference 0.0076 [-0.0232, 0.0360]: on test M1 is *worse*
  than Elo in log loss, within noise.

## Calibration
Validation ECE: EuroLeague 0.043 (Elo 0.057), GBL 0.060 (Elo 0.095). The ten reliability bins
of every split and model are in the reports (`metrics.<split>.<model>.reliability`).

## Heteroscedasticity (R4)
On the tuning seasons the squared margin residual does not grow with expected pace or with the
rating gap: EuroLeague pace slope p = 0.248, gap slope p = 0.264, likelihood-ratio test of a
pace-scaled sigma p = 0.284; GBL p = 0.542, 0.407 and 0.613. This matches R4's weak evidence: a
constant scale is enough, and the pace-scaled GBL variant won on tuning log loss by far less
than the noise. The heavier-tailed Student-t (df 7) is what helps.

## Where M1 loses, limitations and what failed
- **Early rounds (EuroLeague):** validation rounds 1-5 log loss 0.5921 vs Elo 0.5784, and the
  postseason 0.6868 vs 0.6770: the season-start carry-over pulls M1 further toward the mean than
  Elo's reversion. M1 wins later in the regular season (0.5769 vs 0.5818).
- **Lopsided GBL games:** 0.2924 vs Elo 0.2733 on the games Elo gives 75% or more to the
  favourite; M1 wins the balanced ones (0.6802 vs 0.7235). M1's margins for the
  Olympiacos/Panathinaikos blowouts are more cautious than Elo's.
- **Not significant:** no log-loss gain is distinguishable from zero in either competition, on
  validation or on test, and the GBL test goes the other way. M1 is live because the
  pre-registered rule says so (E-g), not because it is proven better.
- **Grid edges:** see above; a wider grid is a post-hoc question for the next model.
- **Possessions:** the 0.44 free-throw weight (0.890 agreement on the sample, target 0.9) and
  ESAKE 2018-19 to 2020-21 totals without team rebounds and turnovers (about +0.8 possessions per
  team); both are level shifts.
- **Live window:** the live M1 fits from 2023-24 (EuroLeague) and 2018-19 (GBL), the seasons
  the daily workflow holds, not from 2007; with the tuned decay older seasons weigh little.
- **One home effect** for all seasons (no special 2020-21 no-fans term); no roster information.
