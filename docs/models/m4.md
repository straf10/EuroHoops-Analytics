# M4: GBL ↔ EuroLeague league translation

Weeks 12–14 (`docs/history/prompts/week-12-14.md`, progress log `reports/week12-14_progress.md`).
Reports: `reports/backtest_m4.json` (backtest and gate), `reports/m4_translation.json` (factors
and team offset). Every decimal below is in the Numbers table with its report path, checked by
`tests/test_model_card_m4.py`. The player crosswalk it stands on is `docs/data/entity.md`.

## What it does
Translates a player's per-100-possession box rates from one league to the other: how much a GBL
season's scoring, rebounding, passing and fouling shrinks or grows in the EuroLeague. It is
scored where it matters for scouting: players who move from the GBL to the EuroLeague, predicted
from their last GBL season before their first EuroLeague one.

## Data and exclusions
- The 11 H-e rates (PTS, 2PA, 3PA, FTA, OREB, DREB, AST, STL, BLK, TOV, PF per 100 possessions),
  player possessions = team possessions × minutes share, as in M3. EuroLeague 2007–2025, GBL
  2018–2025 (box scores before 2020-21 are less reliable, `docs/data/gbl.md`); the live season
  is not used.
- One person per player across the two leagues from the crosswalk (`player_xwalk`).
- Pairs (`models/translation_pairs.py`): **duals** (≥ 300 minutes in both leagues in one season:
  the PAO/OLY players), **GBL→EL movers** (≥ 300 GBL minutes in season s, < 100 EuroLeague
  minutes in s, ≥ 300 in s+1) and **EL→GBL movers** (the reverse). Counts in the Numbers table.

## Method
Per stat k: log((EL rate + c_k)/(GBL rate + c_k)) = δ_k + β_k·dual + u_person + ε, weights the
harmonic mean of the two minutes, a person random effect, variances by REML, δ_k with a 90%
interval (`models/translation.py`). `translate_team` adds the gap in the two teams' net rating
of the season before. Walk forward by EuroLeague target season t: every fit, sd, league mean and
team ratio uses only seasons before t (`eval/m4_backtest.py`).

## Baselines and loss
`same_stats` (the GBL rates as they are; the gate is against it), `team_offset` (PAO/OLY
team-level EL/GBL rate ratios), `shrunk_same_stats` (shrunk toward the EuroLeague mean, prior
weight 250 minutes). Loss per mover: mean over the 11 stats of the squared error in units of the
stat's EuroLeague between-player sd (tuning seasons); split loss weighted by EuroLeague minutes.

## Gate
Rule (I-h): the chosen variant beats `same_stats` iff the person-level bootstrap 95% CI of
loss(chosen) − loss(same_stats) on validation has upper bound < 0. Only one GBL→EL mover falls in
validation (2023), so the pre-registered small-sample rule applied: the gate pools 2019–2023
(6 movers) with `translate` declared, no choice on data.

**Result: FAIL.** loss(translate) − loss(same_stats) = −0.137504, 95% CI [−0.429934, 0.018067].
Translation lowers the loss on average, but six movers cannot show it; the upper bound sits just
above 0. The test seasons (2024–2025) have no GBL→EL mover at all.

## Translation factors (fit for target season 2025: every pair before 2025)
δ is the log ratio EuroLeague / GBL; in the EuroLeague a GBL player keeps about 94% of his
scoring rate, 84% of his assists, 87% of his steals, 90% of his free-throw attempts and 91% of
his defensive rebounds, and commits about 13% more fouls. Shooting volume (2PA, 3PA), offensive
rebounds and turnovers carry over unchanged within their intervals.

| FTA | −0.105476 | [−0.173732, −0.03722] |

## Team offset (I-k)
From PAO and OLY, who play both leagues: the average GBL team is 20.202848 points per 100
possessions below the average EuroLeague team (95% CI [16.856517, 23.641515]), over 14
club-seasons. R19's cross-league gaps are of the same order.

## Runtime
`eurohoops backtest --model m4` runs in about 20 s (budget 600 s, I-l).

## Limitations and what failed
- **The gate has no power.** GBL→EL moves with ≥ 300 EuroLeague minutes are rare (6 in
  2019–2025), so the pre-registered comparison could not reach significance, and test could not
  be scored. The factors themselves are estimated mostly from duals (PAO/OLY players in both
  leagues in one season), which are a different population from movers (role and team strength
  differ).
- The crosswalk's own misses (`docs/data/entity.md`) can drop movers. On the owner's 186 labels
  it made no false match and missed one true one (Vezenkov); M4 runs on the crosswalk as
  frozen (without the override that fixes him), so the pre-registered scores stand.
- No aging term, no minutes projection: a mover's role change is part of the error.
