## What it does
M3 rates every EuroLeague player since 2011-12 by how the scoreboard moves per 100 possessions
while he is on the floor, adjusted for the nine other players and home court (regularised
adjusted plus-minus, RAPM), with an offensive (O) and a defensive (D) part. The chosen variant,
`rapm_spm`, shrinks each player toward a box-score estimate (SPM) instead of toward zero. Team
margins for a game come from those ratings, the players' projected shares of minutes and the
expected possessions, so M3 can be backtested as a game forecaster against M1 and against
box-score baselines that see the same inputs without lineups. It also reports a rating with a
90% interval for every player-season, and tests whether EuroLeague box-score models carry to the
Greek Basket League (GBL). M3 feeds no live prediction and no site page.

## Data and exclusions
- **EuroLeague stints:** the stints mart built from play-by-play (weeks 5–7, checked per game in
  `stint_game_checks`); a game is rated when played and not forfeit.
- **Box scores:** per-player EuroLeague box lines from the raw cache, used for projected minutes,
  the SPM prior and the box-only and PIR baselines.
- **Seasons:** warm-up 2011–2014, tuning 2015–2022 (2236 games), validation 2023 (331 games),
  test 2024–2025 (732 games). Every rated game in a split was scored; none dropped.
- **Walk-forward (H-c):** a game's rating comes only from stints of games that tipped off before
  the first tip-off of its round, and its projected minutes only from earlier games. No
  hyperparameter saw validation or test games.
- **GBL:** the GBL stints built from play-by-play (2018–19 only; there is no GBL play-by-play for
  2023–25) pass all three per-game checks in 0.5614 of games, far below the H-i rule of 0.95,
  almost entirely on possessions (0.5813 in 2018, 0.5755 in 2019; points 1.0000 both seasons).
  So there is no GBL RAPM; the GBL part of M3 is a transfer of EuroLeague box-score models.

## Method
- **Design:** each stint has two sides; each side is one row with the offense five's O columns at
  +1, the defense five's D columns at −1, a home column and an intercept. Target 100 × points /
  possessions, weight possessions × 0.5^(age / half-life). One O and one D column per player
  across teams and seasons.
- **Solve:** the decayed normal equations are kept incrementally (sparse), and at every round
  cutoff the ridge system is solved by conjugate gradients, warm-started from the previous round.
- **SPM prior (D2):** for season s, a ridge regression of plain RAPM ratings at s's first round
  cutoff (every stint strictly before season s, an expanding window) on 11 decayed, shrunk
  per-100 box rates. Within season s, a player's prior is that model applied to his box rates at
  the current cutoff. The first season has no prior (mean 0).
- **Game margin:** expected possessions / 100 × (home-court term + the sum over the ten players of
  projected share × (O − D)), each side's share from recent box minutes.
- **Posterior (H4, H7):** the ridge solution is the posterior mean of a Gaussian model; the
  posterior covariance is noise variance × (Gram + penalty)⁻¹. On 200 synthetic seasons, 90%
  intervals cover 88.38% of the true ratings (`models/rapm_posterior.py` tests).

## Variants and the search
Three declared RAPM variants (H6), all tuned on the tuning seasons by future-margin RMSE, the
lowest one chosen before any validation number existed (verdict commit b09d19a):

| Variant | Tuning RMSE | Chosen parameters |
|---|---|---|
| **rapm_spm** (chosen) | **11.723402** | half-life 5840 days, ridge 4000 (O and D), SPM α 1.0, k 250 minutes; SPM targets from plain RAPM with half-life 5840, ridge 2000 |
| rapm | 11.730621 | half-life 5840, ridge 2000 (48-point grid; separate O and D ridges were not better) |
| rapm_dummy | 11.736241 | low-minute spells pooled per team-season below 50 minutes |

The `rapm_spm` ridge search ran from 250 (12.030610) to 8000 (11.774897), with 2000 (11.723806)
and 4000 (11.723402) almost level. `rapm_dummy`'s threshold got worse as it rose: 100 minutes
11.747105, 200 minutes 11.792828, 400 minutes 11.851821. The RAPM half-life of 5840 days is the
grid top but longer than the whole fit window, so it means almost no decay.

Comparison models, fitted on the tuning seasons only:
- `box_only`: the same 11 box rates, decayed and shrunk per player, weighted by projected shares
  (half-life 182 days, k 250, ridge 300 by leave-one-season-out CV) — the lineup-free baseline
  the gate compares against.
- `pir`: the same construction on the PIR rating alone.
- `m1`: the committed M1 team-efficiency model, replayed, not re-tuned.
- `b0`: the tuning-season home margin, 3.867 points.

## Greek Basket League transfer (H8)
With no usable GBL stints, H8 asks whether the EuroLeague SPM (box rates → player O/D) predicts
GBL games. At each GBL round cutoff it uses the SPM of the latest EuroLeague season whose fit
time (that season's first round cutoff, data strictly before it) is at or before the cutoff, on
GBL box rates built the same way, with GBL projected minutes and possessions. Two forms:
`spm_transfer` fits only a home coefficient on the GBL tuning games (2021–2022); in
`spm_transfer_scaled` a scale factor on the EuroLeague margin is fitted too (2.43: EuroLeague
ratings are far too compressed for GBL games). GBL `box_only`, `pir`, `m1` and `b0` are fitted
on GBL data. Splits: tuning 336 games, validation 163 (2023), test 340 (2024–2025), none dropped,
the same games per season as `reports/backtest_m1_gbl.json`.

| Model | Validation RMSE | Validation log loss | Test RMSE | Test log loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| spm_transfer | 14.454 | 0.5647 | 13.304 | 0.5655 |
| spm_transfer_scaled | 12.714 | 0.4828 | 12.155 | 0.4952 |
| box_only (GBL) | 12.715 | 0.4883 | 11.926 | 0.4850 |
| pir (GBL) | 13.438 | 0.5259 | 12.093 | 0.4978 |
| m1 (GBL) | **11.734** | **0.4313** | **11.919** | **0.4845** |
| b0 | 16.585 | 0.6599 | 15.608 | 0.6670 |

**EuroLeague player models do not beat GBL-native models in the GBL.** The unscaled transfer
is worse than GBL box-only by 1.377443 points of test RMSE (95% CI [0.727102, 2.010212]) and
worse than M1 by 1.384891 [0.776675, 1.945499]. Scaling closes most of the gap to box-only but not
to M1. This is GBL evidence, not the exit gate.

## Player ratings and uncertainty (H7)
`reports/m3_players.json`: 4577 player-seasons, 1727 players, `rapm_spm` at each season's last
round cutoff — the walk-forward state fitted on every stint before that round's first tip-off,
the object that predicts the season's last round. Each row has O, D and O + D (points per 100
possessions), their posterior sds and 90% intervals (O + D includes the O/D covariance), minutes,
games and teams. Noise variance comes from the tuning fit's residuals at the 2022 snapshot
(127688 stint rows, degrees of freedom = the 2927 columns used). A player not yet seen by any
stint sits at his prior with the prior's sd. The median sd of O + D falls from 2.55 (2011) to
about 2.2 (2021–2025) as the fit accumulates seasons, and sd falls with minutes (Spearman
−0.753). The 2019 snapshot is the scheduled but cancelled round 34 (2020-04-09), so it holds every
2019-20 game played. `minutes` counts the whole season's box minutes, including the last round
the rating does not see.

Highest O + D in 2025 among players with 600+ minutes (source player codes):

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Runtime
`eurohoops backtest --model m3 --score-test` (the recorded run, H-j) took 875 s against the
1800 s budget; two consecutive runs are byte-identical and equal the committed report. The GBL
backtest takes about 72 s and `eurohoops m3-players` about 101 s, each byte-identical across two
runs.

## Limitations and what failed
- **The validation edge did not hold on test.** The gate passed narrowly on one season; on the
  two test seasons `rapm_spm` ties box-only and M1 is slightly better. M3 is not a better game
  forecaster than M1 on the evidence here; treat 2026-27 as the confirmation before any M3 goes
  live.
- **No GBL RAPM:** GBL possession ends look under-detected (0.5614 of games pass all checks), and
  there is no GBL play-by-play for 2023–25.
- **Transfer fails in the GBL:** EuroLeague box-score player models are worse than GBL box-only
  and M1; the fitted scale factor says EuroLeague ratings do not carry at face value.
- **Near-flat choices:** `rapm_spm` ridges 2000 and 4000 differ in the fourth decimal of tuning
  RMSE, and `rapm_spm` vs plain `rapm` in the third.
- **Wide player intervals** (median sd of O + D about 2.2 per 100): rankings within the middle of
  the league are noise.
- Player codes are the EuroLeague API's source codes; names are not in the report.
