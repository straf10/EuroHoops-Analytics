## What it does
Projects each player's per-100-possession rates (points, 3-point and free-throw attempts, assists,
turnovers, offensive and defensive rebounds, steals, blocks), his TS%, 3P% and FT%, and in the
EuroLeague his impact (SPM and the BRAPM posterior mean from M3), each with an 80% interval: for
next season, and for the rest of a season from 25%, 50% or 75% of its rounds. Minutes are not
projected (M5's `minutes.py` owns them). Around the projections: an over/under board (who is
shooting or impacting above what should last), "plays like" comparables, and a list of young GBL
players whose projected game would translate well to the EuroLeague.

## Method
- **Input** (`models/player_seasons.py`): one row per person, competition and season from the box
  scores of the regular season and the 2007-2015 Top 16 (never the knockouts), possessions as
  exposure, persons joined across the two leagues by the entity crosswalk.
- **Projection** (`models/projection.py`): per stat, the player's seasons weighted by an
  exponential season decay (half-life on a grid) and by exposure, shrunk to the league prior of the
  three previous seasons by an empirical-Bayes weight (possessions for counts, attempts for
  percentages); the predictive variance adds the posterior variance, the season-to-season drift and
  the sampling noise of the target season. BRAPM snapshots enter with the noise and drift of the
  snapshot series (lag-1/lag-2 moments, decision D17).
- **Variants** (chosen on tuning only, L-i): `proj_shrunk` (decay + shrinkage), `proj_age` (+ a
  delta-method aging curve on regressed season-s rates, survivor corrected, `models/aging.py`),
  `proj_full` (+ M4's league translation for movers), `proj_full_spm` (the projected SPM as BRAPM's
  prior). **Baselines:** `same_as_last`, `league_mean`, `marcel` (5/4/3 weights regressed by 1,200
  possessions with Marcel's age factor).
- **Calibration** (D19): the chosen cell's predictive sd is multiplied per stat and checkpoint by
  the square root of the mean squared standardised error of its own tuning forecasts, frozen at the
  verdict. Means and the loss do not change.
- **Loss** (L-g): per player, the squared errors of the stats standardised by a fixed
  between-player SD from the seasons before the first tuning target, summed, possession-weighted
  over players with at least 500 possessions in the target and a senior season before it.

## Evaluation
- **Splits:** EuroLeague tuning 2016-17 to 2022-23, validation 2023-24, test 2024-25 and 2025-26;
  each target season sees nothing from its own games or later (`tests/test_m6_leakage.py`, planted
  leaks caught). Order in git: verdict, then validation, then test (`scripts/checks/m6_order.py`).
- **Gate** (validation 2023-24, point rule): the chosen variant's loss below `marcel`'s and
  `same_as_last`'s, and the pooled tuning + validation 80% coverage inside 75–85% for every stat.

## Results
**Choice: `proj_shrunk` with a half-life of 1 season** (tuning loss 6.090287; half-life 0.5 gave
6.249258 and 2 gave 6.092360, so the choice is inside the widened grid). Aging and translation did
not help on tuning: `proj_age` 6.169617, `proj_full` 6.166064, `proj_full_spm` 6.168281, against
`marcel` 6.190196.

the short decay, not from aging or translation.

**Rest of season** (reported, not gated): the chosen cell is below `marcel` at every checkpoint of

**Movers** (GBL to EuroLeague and back, a slice of the next-season targets): too few to read
(6 on tuning, 2 on validation, 2 on test); on test 7.258933 vs `marcel` 9.229818.

**GBL** (reported; the EuroLeague verdict as a fixed choice; tuning 2019-20 to 2022-23): ties
`marcel` on tuning (−0.092705, CI −0.242576 to 0.063066) and test (0.024464, CI −0.206626 to
0.235886), worse on the 53 validation players (6.314935 vs 5.951288). Blocks (0.867987) and 3P%
(0.881944) over-cover their 80% intervals. The GBL has no impact stats.

## Live
`uv run eurohoops project` (`src/eurohoops/live_m6.py`) projects every player with a 2026-27 line
with the verdict cell, at the largest checkpoint whose rounds are complete (decision D22: next
season until 25% of the rounds, so projections change four times a season). The daily workflow runs
it (`--projections-only`) because the gate passed; it reads the committed crosswalk
`entity/player_xwalk.csv`. The board and the comparables are built locally from the newest complete
season (2025-26) and do not move during the season. `uv run eurohoops sim-ungated` writes the
Standings page's season simulation (M7's run, labelled "not gated": M7 failed its gate).

- **Over/under board** (`models/board.py`): shot-making (points above M2's xPTS per 100 attempts),
  3P% against a prior from FT%, and on/off net rating against BRAPM, each with a z score and the
  share of the gap expected to persist from its year-to-year stability on tuning seasons (shot-making
  0.3721, 3P% 0.2987, on/off 0.0431: almost none of an on/off gap lasts).
- **"Plays like"** (`models/similarity.py`): nearest player-seasons by standardised box rates and,
  in the EuroLeague, shot zones and shot-making; a person never matches himself.
- **GBL undervalued** (D24): at most 3 seasons since debut, under 20 minutes a game, projected
  rates translated to the EuroLeague giving an SPM in the top quarter of GBL rotation players.

## Limitations
- Minutes are not projected; injuries and roster changes are not known to the model.
- Newcomers without a usable season (most live-season arrivals from outside both leagues) get the
  league prior and the `no_history` flag; their intervals are wide by construction.
- Players without a known age take the no-aging path; the chosen variant does not age anyone.
- BRAPM is EuroLeague-only and starts in 2011-12; the GBL list's SPM is the EuroLeague SPM on
  translated rates, never a GBL impact measurement.
- M4's translation factors failed their own gate; they enter only the GBL list and `proj_full`.
