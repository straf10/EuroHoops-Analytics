## What it does
Simulates the rest of a regular season many times and turns the simulated final tables into
probabilities: each team's final-place distribution, P(direct playoffs), P(play-in), P(top 10),
and through the knockouts P(Final Four) and P(title). Each simulation first draws every team's
strength from M1's posterior (so the uncertainty about how good a team is, not only game-to-game
noise, spreads the outcomes), keeps it for the whole season, plays every remaining fixture with
M1's margin model, ranks the table with the official tie-breaks, and plays the play-in, playoffs
and Final Four (GBL: quarterfinals, semifinals, final).

## Method
- **Strength posterior** (`models/team_eff.py` `rating_posteriors`): M1's ridge on team-game
  offensive ratings is read as a Gaussian posterior over [μ, h, off, def]: mean = the ridge solution
  at the cutoff, covariance = σ²·(G + P)⁻¹, with G the decayed, possession-weighted Gram matrix, P the
  ridge penalty and σ² the ridge residual variance with the effective degrees of freedom subtracted
  (decision D7). On 200 synthetic leagues its 90% intervals cover the truth within 87–93%. Pace is
  M1's pace fit at the cutoff, a point estimate. M1's committed parameters are used, not re-tuned.
- **Games** (`sim/season.py`): margin = pace·(ORtg_H − ORtg_A)/100 + noise, noise M1's Student-t
  (EuroLeague df 7), rounded; a 0 becomes ±1 (overtime is not modelled, and overtime points count
  for no tie-break). Scores follow from the expected total, so the score tie-breaks have scores.
- **Tables** (`standings.rank`, plus `rank_by_wins` for speed): the exact EuroLeague Art. 19
  procedure with deductions. It reproduces the official final table of all 8 scored EuroLeague
  seasons from the games mart (overtime games on their regulation score).
- **Formats** (`sim/formats.py`): per season, with sources. Bylaws exist online only for 2025-26 and
  2026-27; earlier cut lines come from the official final tables and the playoff/play-in fields in
  the EuroLeague's games feed (`scripts/checks/m7_facts.py`).

## Evaluation
- **Checkpoints:** after 25%, 50% and 75% of a season's rounds (cutoff = the next round's first
  tip-off); everything a checkpoint uses comes from games before it (`tests/test_m7_leakage.py`,
  with two planted leaks caught). 4,000 simulations per checkpoint, seed 20261101 + checkpoint index.
- **Targets:** the real final table's direct-playoff cut (top 8 through 2022-23, top 6 after), the
  top 10 from 2023-24, and the final place.
- **Variants** (chosen on tuning only, by the lowest RPS of the final place): `sim_full` (posterior
  draws, M1's full noise), `sim_net` (noise shrunk so strength + noise variance equals M1's
  predictive variance), `sim_inflate` (sim_net with the covariance × 1.5 or 2). **Baselines:**
  `point_sim` (M1 point strengths, no strength uncertainty), `elo_sim` (comparison-Elo strengths),
  `standings_now` (the current table as the final one).
- **Gate** (validation 2023-24): the chosen variant's Brier of the direct cut below both
  `point_sim`'s and `standings_now`'s, and the pooled tuning + validation Spiegelhalter |z| < 1.96.

**Gate: FAIL.** On validation `sim_full` beats `standings_now` but not `point_sim` (Brier
difference +0.003675, team-cluster bootstrap 95% CI −0.003868 to +0.010623, spanning 0); the pooled
z (−0.870358) is well inside 1.96, so the failure is the point rule, not calibration. `point_sim`
is ahead at each validation checkpoint (25%: 0.127834 vs 0.122470; 75%: 0.046499 vs 0.045464). On
test the order flips: `sim_full` − `point_sim` = −0.000939 (CI −0.007556 to +0.004971). Read
together: drawing strengths from M1's posterior neither helps nor hurts the direct-cut Brier
measurably on 54 validation and 114 test team-checkpoints; the simulator is calibrated (|z| < 1.96
in every EuroLeague split) and far better than the current table (test: −0.094603, CI −0.170126 to
−0.033202). PLAN §5.8's warning that point strengths are overconfident shows on tuning
(`point_sim` z 1.38 vs `sim_full` −0.42) and in the GBL, not as a Brier gap in the EuroLeague.

**Reliability** (test, `sim_full`): the predicted shares follow the observed ones bin by bin
(`backtest_m7.json` `reliability.test.sim_full`), e.g. 44 team-checkpoints below 0.1 with none made.

**Title odds** (reported, not gated): mean Brier over the teams' P(title), summed over teams per
checkpoint: `sim_full` 1.126624 on validation, 0.887707 on test (`elo_sim` 0.866727 on test).

**GBL** (reported; the EuroLeague verdict as a fixed choice; tuning 2020-21–2022-23, test 2025-26):
on tuning `sim_full` has Brier 0.103094 against `point_sim`'s 0.108201, and `point_sim` is
miscalibrated (z 2.771) while `sim_full` is not (z 0.924); on the one test season (13 teams) every
model is miscalibrated (`sim_full` z 3.016). GBL rules are unverified (playoff places, series
lengths, tie-breaks) and carried into every GBL output.

## Gate v2 (declared 2026-10-09 before any run, PLAN §8.1 R5)
v1's verdict rests on one validation season (54 team-checkpoints). v2 re-tests the same question
on every usable season. It is **not a clean hold-out**: every scored season was already seen by
v1 (tuning, validation 2023-24 or test). It is a larger-sample re-evaluation, run once.
- **Seasons:** EuroLeague 2016-17 to 2025-26 that the format table admits (2019-20 and 2021-22
  stay excluded). 2016-17 is the first selection season only; scored: 2017-18, 2018-19, 2020-21,
  2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26 (372 team-checkpoints).
- **Rolling origin, expanding window:** for each scored season, the variant (`sim_full`,
  `sim_net`, `sim_inflate` × 1.5 / 2) is chosen by the lowest pooled RPS of the final place on all
  earlier eligible seasons, then that season is scored. M1's committed parameters are used
  unchanged (shared by every variant and `point_sim`, so the comparison between them is fair).
  Checkpoints, targets, simulation counts and seeds as in v1.
- **Baselines:** `point_sim` and `standings_now`, as v1. No swap of the published model after the
  fact.
- **Pass** requires all three:
  1. non-inferiority to `point_sim`: the pooled Brier difference of the direct cut (chosen
     variant − `point_sim`) has a season-cluster bootstrap 95% CI (10,000 resamples of whole
     seasons, fixed seed) with upper bound below **+5 thousandths** of Brier (declared margin);
  2. pooled Brier below `standings_now`'s;
  3. calibration: pooled Spiegelhalter |z| < 1.96 over all scored team-checkpoints.
- **Effect:** PASS puts `simulate` in the daily workflow and the Standings page drops the "not
  gated" label; FAIL keeps both as they are.
- **GBL:** not gated. The EuroLeague verdict applies as a fixed choice, labelled "not gated, GBL
  rules unverified".

## Live
`uv run eurohoops simulate [--competition gbl] [--dry-run]` (`src/eurohoops/live_sim.py`) simulates
the 2026-27 regular season after its completed rounds (10,000 simulations, the verdict variant) and
appends one row per team to `predictions/{competition}_sim_2026-27.csv`, once per completed round,
**only when the EuroLeague gate passed**. It failed, so the command is built and checked (checklist
item 52) but not in the daily workflow, and no simulation log exists.

## Limitations
- Strengths are fixed within a simulation (no form, injuries or roster changes after the cutoff).
- Overtime is not modelled; a tied regulation margin is a coin flip.
- M1's decay is a fractional likelihood, so the posterior describes the decayed average strength;
  the inflation variants that would widen it did worse on tuning.
- A team with no game yet (a promoted GBL club before round 1) starts at the average strength of
  the teams that left the league, as promotion replaces relegation (owner, 2026-10-07), with M1's
  wide prior variance; its odds stay wide until it plays.
- GBL formats and tie-breaks are unverified; two GBL seasons are excluded.
- Small samples: the gate rests on one validation season (18 teams × 3 checkpoints).
