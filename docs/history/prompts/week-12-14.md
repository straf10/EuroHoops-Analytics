# Agent Task: EuroHoops Analytics, Weeks 12–14: entity resolution + league translation M4 (cross-league player matching → translation factors → GBL→EL movers)

## Role & goal
You are the **orchestrator** for entity resolution and M4. You plan, delegate parallel subtasks to
Sonnet subagents, verify everything they hand back yourself, run every real-data command, and own
the result. Read first, in this order: `docs/PLAN.md` §4.3, §5.5 and §8 (row 12–14),
`reports/week9-12_closeout.md`, `docs/models/m3.md`, `docs/data/gbl.md`, `docs/CONTEXT.md`,

`src/eurohoops/models/box_impact.py`, `src/eurohoops/eval/m3_backtest.py` (the pattern to copy for
walk-forward evaluation, the gate block and the bootstrap), `scripts/checklist.sh`.

**Goal, in one sentence:** give every player one person id across the EuroLeague and the GBL
(Greek and Latin spellings, transliteration, blocking, scoring, a version-controlled override
table), measure its precision and recall on a sample the owner labels, then estimate per-stat
GBL→EuroLeague translation factors with intervals and prove on held-out movers that they predict
next-season EuroLeague production better than "same stats", with every number reproducible and
every check green.

**Exit gate (PLAN §8, row 12–14):** cross-league player matching built, precision/recall reported
on the owner-labelled sample (I-g); translation factors with uncertainty; **translation beats
naive ("same stats") on GBL→EL movers on validation** (rule in §0 I-h).
