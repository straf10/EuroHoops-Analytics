**Goal, in one sentence:** build a roster-aware game predictor whose team strength is the sum of
projected minutes × player rating, plus a team residual, home court and rest, blended with M1 and
Elo and calibrated, that outputs P(win), an expected margin and an expected total with full
predictive distributions (so over/under probabilities for any line), and prove on EuroLeague
validation, with the test seasons scored once afterwards, whether it beats M1, with every number
reproducible and every check green.

**Exit gate (PLAN §8, row 14–16, M5 half):** M5 (projected roster, the variant chosen on tuning)
beats M1 on EuroLeague validation 2023 by the rule in §0 J-h. The oracle-roster variant is
reported next to it as an upper bound, clearly labelled, and never gated, chosen or logged live.

**M7 (season simulator) is not part of this file.** It gets its own prompt after M5 closes out.
